# STRATEGY — the money & platform playbook

> How the studio makes money and stays alive on YouTube + TikTok. These are **build-time rules**, not
> afterthoughts — several are existential (break them and the whole network gets terminated). Sourced from
> 2026 research; revisit at phase boundaries. Priorities/rules feed [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md).

## 0. The one reframe that changes the economics

**Do NOT build "news/politics" channels — build FINANCE / BUSINESS / TECH news.** Generic news & politics
is among the *worst*-monetized niches (thin fill, weak RPM); finance/business RPM is ~$12–$35 vs. news's
low single digits — a 3–7× swing for the *same* production pipeline. We keep news's freshness and
trend-velocity while capturing finance-tier RPM.

**→ RULE (niche gate):** every channel targets a finance/business/tech-adjacent news vertical (RPM floor
≥ ~$10). No pure hard-news/politics channels.

## 1. Revenue model — a STACK, never one stream

News *ad* RPM is weak; ads alone underperform. Creators with 3+ streams earn ~$75k/yr more. Target mix:
**sponsorships 30–50% · ads 20–40% · affiliate/products 10–30% · memberships 5–10%**, plus TikTok Creator
Rewards and **clip licensing** (viral news clips sold B2B — underused, high-margin, face-free = easy to
license).

- **YouTube long-form = the money** (earns ~20× per view vs Shorts). **Shorts/TikTok = funnel + secondary.**
- **TikTok RPM ~10× YouTube Shorts** → post to TikTok for pay, YT Shorts for reach.
- Cost is ~fixed (our own farm) → every monetized minute is near-pure margin. The game is *consistent
  publishing across a portfolio*, not squeezing one video.

**→ RULE:** each channel wires ≥3 revenue streams by the 500-sub mark (memberships + affiliate + a
sponsorship slot) and feeds the clip-licensing export. **→ RULE:** Shorts are a funnel — optimize for
subs→long-form conversion, never treat Short views as the revenue target.

## 2. Monetization thresholds (the finish lines to design toward)

- **YouTube YPP** — Tier 1 (fan funding) 500 subs + 3 uploads + 3k watch-hrs *or* 3M Shorts views/90d.
  Tier 2 (**ad revenue**) 1,000 subs + **4,000 watch-hrs/12mo** *or* 10M Shorts views/90d. Needs AdSense,
  2FA, zero strikes. Ramp ≈ **3–5 uploads/week**.
- **TikTok Creator Rewards** — **10k followers + 100k authentic views/30d**, videos **≥1 min** (sub-60s
  earns $0), qualified view = ≥5s, payout $10/mo. Finance/tech + **>70% completion** → RPM $2.50–$6
  (Additional Reward multiplier); generic ~$0.40–$1.
- **Ramp reality:** plan **6–12 months at a loss** before monetization. Fund the runway; don't kill
  channels early. Q4 (Nov–Dec) RPM spikes; January −40% — front-load big pushes into Q4.

**→ Per-account KPI:** track subs/watch-hours (YT) and followers/100k-views/completion (TikTok) as the
graduation gate before an account counts as a revenue asset.

## 3. COMPLIANCE — the survival layer (breaking these kills the network)

This is the existential part. YouTube's **Inauthentic Content policy** (Jul 2025) makes mass-produced,
templated, low-variation AI content **ineligible for monetization at the CHANNEL level** — one violation
can demonetize the whole channel. The 2025–26 crackdown terminated thousands of AI channels / billions of
views. Survival = **transformative editorial value + human gate + disclosure + variation.**

**Hard pipeline gates (auto-fail if not met):**
1. **Original analysis, always.** Every long-form adds commentary/analysis/context — **never narrate wire
   copy verbatim.** Verbatim = demonetization risk. TikTok money-format must be original-scored too.
2. **Human editorial review before every publish.** We operate as *editorial director using AI as a
   production tool*, not "AI creator+producer." Satisfies the policy's line.
3. **AI disclosure every time.** Toggle YouTube "altered/synthetic content" + TikTok "AI-generated";
   embed C2PA credentials. Disclosure does **not** reduce reach/monetization — **non-disclosure is the
   risk** (auto-labeled + fast-tracked demonetization). Log disclosure state per asset.
4. **Fact / defamation gate.** Any factual claim about a **named real person/company** → 2-source
   verification + human sign-off before publish (publisher is liable; AI hallucinates 26–41%; you cannot
   delegate editorial responsibility). Log the check.
5. **Hard content ban:** no synthetic depiction of **real people/newsmakers**, no fabricated statements by
   public figures, no election/crisis synthetic media (banned even with a label). **AI news = original
   commentary/summary over stock/B-roll/licensed footage — not deepfaked newsmakers.** Our own fictional
   anchor (NNS talent) is fine; a synthetic real official is a ban.
6. **No batch-identical output.** Enforce variation in structure, hook, thumbnail, framing across videos;
   rotate ≥4–5 templates. Single-template mass production trips the inauthentic-content policy.
7. **Sensitive-topic framing:** non-graphic discussion/analysis retains monetization under Jan-2026
   guidelines; auto-block child-abuse / eating-disorder topics entirely.

## 4. Packaging & growth targets (what the pipeline must hit)

**YouTube:** optimize **CTR × AVD ("Quality CTR")**, not CTR alone (high CTR + low retention is demoted).
Targets: **CTR 6–8%, AVD 40–55%, 0:30 retention >65–70%.** Every long-form open hits the **3-phase hook**
(pattern-interrupt 0–5s → payoff promise 5–15s → commitment 15–30s), intro <10s, result first. Cadence
**1–3 long-form/week + 3–5 Shorts/week** on a fixed, anticipatable schedule; satisfaction > volume.

**TikTok:** **70% completion in first 3s → 2.2× views** (>85% = viral tier, <60% = suppressed). Money
format **60–90s, retention-optimized** with mid-video re-hooks; sub-60s only for growth. Cadence **1–3/day
per account, jittered**; **3–5 vetted hashtags** (banned-tag check); library/original audio only.

**Cross-posting (avoid the watermark tax):** render a **watermark-free master**, emit **per-platform
variants** (distinct first frame + audio + caption style) — TikTok-watermarked reposts to YT/IG are
downranked 40–70% and hit reused-content demonetization.

## 5. Portfolio & account strategy (scale without cascade risk)

**Sequential scaling:** Phase 1 = **one flagship channel to profitability**; then **clone the workflow**
to 2–3; expand only after a channel is cash-flow positive. Realistic target **2–3 channels → $5–10k/mo in
12–18 months** (not 20 channels day one).

**Identity isolation (critical):** one Google identity ≈ one AdSense identity — a severe strike can
demonetize *all* linked channels. **Cap ~3–5 channels per Google/AdSense identity; isolate identities per
risk cluster** so a termination can't cascade. Differentiate channels by **vertical/format/voice (real
editorial variation), not template clones** (clones trip inauthentic-content).

**TikTok accounts:** **1 antidetect profile + 1 dedicated mobile proxy per account** (≤3 accts/port,
never simultaneous), **14-day warm-up state machine** (engage-only → 1/day → ramp) before the money pool,
**one niche per account**, jittered schedules (never same-cron-tick across accounts — coordination signal).
Treat portfolio accounts as replaceable.

**→ WORK ITEM:** maintain an **identity/isolation map** (channel → Brand Account → Google/AdSense → GCP
project; TikTok account → profile → proxy) + a per-identity strike/health dashboard.

## 6. The AI showrunner (roadmap — after the pipeline ships)

**Orchestrator + specialists**, heterogeneous by cost: **qwen9b (amd0)** for high-volume topic
scoring/classification/drafting; **DeepSeek** for editorial planning + fact-review. Plan-and-execute
(~90% cheaper than frontier-for-everything). The closed loop:

`ingest (Google Trends · YT/TikTok trending · Analytics API · news wires/RSS) → score topics (velocity ×
niche-RPM fit × competition gap) → produce (script→voice→render) → measure (CTR·AVD·retention·RPM) →
learn (correlate → update hooks/lengths/thumbnails/topic strategy)`

**Bounded autonomy:** the showrunner **owns** topic ranking, scheduling, thumbnail/title A/B, killing
underperformers, format iteration within limits. It **ESCALATES** to human: sensitive-category factual
claims, legal/defamation-risk topics, new-channel launches, spend above threshold. Every decision logged
(audit trail). This is a **scale lever, built after** the manual pipeline is locked and shipping.

## 7. APIs & automation limits (design within these)

- **YouTube Data API:** 10,000 units/day/**project**; upload now ~100 units (~100 uploads/day),
  `search.list` scarce (~100/day, own bucket). **Shard channels across multiple GCP projects**; drive
  topic discovery from **external news feeds, not API search**. **Analytics API** feeds the showrunner loop.
- **TikTok Content Posting API:** Direct Post, **25 videos/day/account, 6 req/min**, and **public posting
  requires a TikTok audit (~5–10 business days)** — start it before launch. API for audited flagship
  accounts; antidetect-browser automation for disposable portfolio accounts — **never mix
  credentials/fingerprints**.
